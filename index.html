What Makes It Special

It doesn't just suggest — it acts

Most AI tools give you advice. Vera's "agent" actually does the task: it thinks, takes a real action, checks the result, and repeats — like a smart helper that follows through.

💭 ThinkUnderstand what you need
→
⚡ ActCreate a task, send a reply…
→
👀 ObserveCheck the result

✅ ResolveFinish or keep going

And because it can take real actions, Vera asks for your approval before anything sensitive — you always stay in control.
